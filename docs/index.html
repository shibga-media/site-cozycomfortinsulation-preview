Blown-In Insulation

Loose-fill cellulose or fiberglass blown evenly over the attic floor and into closed cavities, with no tearing out of walls or ceilings.

Spray Foam Insulation

Common questions

How do I know if my insulation needs to be replaced?

Common signs are rooms that are too hot or cold, high energy bills, drafts, uneven temperatures between floors, or insulation that is wet, flattened, or has been disturbed by pests. We inspect it and tell you plainly what you need.

Is the estimate really free?

Yes. Tell us about your home in the chat or call us, and we will give you a free, no-obligation estimate.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. Cozy Comfort Insulation holds California CSLB C-2 (insulation and acoustical) licence #1140834 and carries general liability and workers' compensation insurance.

Do I need to leave home during the work?

Usually not. Most attic and blown-in jobs are done in a day with you at home. We will tell you ahead of time if a job needs anything different.
